## Sessions you can return to

- Blocks now have readable generated names, with an editable **Session name** field above intentions.
- Timer checkpoints save every second and on pause or quit. After restarting, recover a saved block or start a new one while keeping the saves.
- Use `tlock sessions` to list saves, `tlock --resume NAME` to recover one, or `tlock --name NAME` to name a new block.
- Recovery opens paused at the last checkpoint. Time spent outside Tlock does not count as work.
- Switching timers, changing presets, or resetting an active block asks whether to keep working, finish early, or end the session. Early finishes record actual elapsed time and offer a reflection.
- Press `P` for 25/5, 50/10, or 60/10 focus rhythms. Press `F` to finish a block early.
- The header shows today's focus time and completed blocks. Custom countdowns stay standalone after completion.

## Reading and sound

- Choose Qur'an, original Tlock reminders, or Off for reading cards. Show them during every block or only during breaks.
- Cards rotate every 1–60 minutes, avoid immediate repeats, and optionally play a short book animation.
- Verse excerpts are labeled and references are documented in `docs/READING.md`.
- Settings now includes a **Preview** button for the selected sound pack. Preview also works when completion sounds are muted and does not save changes.

The previous two-column layout and short command bar are retained.
Existing history is preserved; the database adds session names, completion reasons, and checkpoints.

### Update

```powershell
tlock update
tlock update --status
```

Reopen Tlock after the background update finishes. A sudden shutdown can lose roughly the final second of timer progress; normal exits save immediately.
