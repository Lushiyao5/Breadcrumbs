# Reading cards

Qur'an cards contain Arabic and short English meanings. Partial verses are
explicitly marked **excerpt**. The cards are reminders, not a substitute for
reading the full passage and its context.

References:

| Theme | Passage |
| --- | --- |
| Ease alongside hardship | [Ash-Sharh 94:5](https://quran.com/en/ash-sharh/5) |
| Seeking knowledge | [Taha 20:114](https://quran.com/en/ta-ha/114) |
| Capacity and responsibility | [Al-Baqarah 2:286](https://quran.com/en/al-baqarah/286) |
| Patience | [Al-Baqarah 2:153](https://quran.com/en/al-baqarah/153) |
| Remembrance | [Al-Baqarah 2:152](https://quran.com/en/al-baqarah/152) |
| Trust | [At-Talaq 65:3](https://quran.com/en/at-talaq/3) |
| Effort | [An-Najm 53:39](https://quran.com/en/an-najm/39) |
| Hope in mercy | [Az-Zumar 39:53](https://quran.com/en/az-zumar/53) |

The new English cards for 53:39 and the excerpt from 39:53 follow Dr. Mustafa
Khattab's *The Clear Quran* wording shown on Quran.com. Existing cards use
short English meanings. Verse references and excerpt boundaries are stored in
`breadcrumbs/verses.py`; the card selector is in `breadcrumbs/reading.py`.

Quote cards are original Breadcrumbs reminders. They are not attributed to historical
figures or presented as religious text.

Reading is offline. Source links are documentation only; the app does not
request verse or quote content from a service.
